Hey! I'm Mickey 👋

Ever since I was a kid, I've been obsessed with making stuff move and look awesome. Art and music were my jam, so becoming a motion designer just made sense.

I learned the ropes at Ariel University and the Israeli Animation College, but honestly? The real learning happens when you're knee-deep in a crazy project at 3 AM wondering "can I make this explode in a cooler way?"

These days I spend my time turning ideas into pixels that pop, designing stuff that makes people go "whoa," and occasionally making things explode (digitally, of course 💥). Whether it's slick motion graphics, eye-catching animations, or audio that hits just right – I'm all in.

Need someone to bring your wild ideas to life? Let's make something awesome together!
