TikTok-style auto-captions for After Effects.
Transcribes your voice-over locally — no cloud, no account, no per-minute fees — and generates animated, word-accurate caption layers in one click.

⬇  Download TikTalk View on GitHub

Free & open source · Windows · After Effects CC 2019+

Install in 4 steps

No certificates, no ZXP wrestling, no admin rights. For real.

1

Download & extract

Hit the download button and extract the ZIP anywhere you like.

2

Double-click install.bat

That's it. It registers the panel for your user — no admin needed.

3

Restart After Effects

Open it from Window > Extensions > TikTalk.

4

Download the engine

On first launch click Download engine in the panel — it fetches whisper + ffmpeg + a language model (~250 MB, once).

💡

Prefer a .zxp? Run setup\build_zxp.ps1 to build a self-signed dist\TikTalk.zxp and install it with any ZXP installer.

How it works

🎙️

Transcribe

Select your audio/video layer and hit Transcribe. Word timings get snapped to the actual waveform.

→
✏️

Edit & style

The transcript is fully editable. Pick any font with the live-preview picker, set colors, stroke, position, reveal mode.

→
✨

Generate Titles

One click → animated caption layers, auto-centered and auto-shrunk to stay in frame.

Why it slaps

🧠

100% local AI

Powered by whisper.cpp on your machine. No cloud, no account, no per-minute fees — your audio never leaves your PC.

⏱️

Word-accurate timing

Every word's keyframe lands exactly when it's spoken — timings snapped to the real waveform, nudgeable by ±1 frame.

🎛️

One layer, hold keyframes

A single "Captions" text layer driven by hold keyframes — clean timeline, easy to restyle, auto-fit to frame.

💥

Animation presets

Pop-in, word-by-word, bounce drop, typewriter and karaoke highlight — or drop in your own custom expression.

🔤

Searchable font picker

Browse every font installed on your machine with live preview (AE 2024+ Font API).

🆓

Free, forever

Open source on GitHub. Captions that pass the vibe check, no cap.
