AESCRIPTS

free tools for editors & motion designers, by Mickey Perry

Open source extensions for After Effects and Premiere Pro. No subscriptions, no license keys, no nonsense — download, run the installer, get back to work.

Extensions

💬

TIKTALK

AFTER EFFECTS

TikTok-style auto-captions. Transcribes your voice-over locally (whisper.cpp — no cloud, no fees) and generates animated, word-accurate caption layers in one click.

Check it out →
🎩

MR. BIN

PREMIERE PRO

Your project panel’s personal butler. One click and he tidies everything into bins by media type — video, audio, images, PSD/AI, MOGRTs, sequences. Full undo, naturally.

Check it out →
🎲

RANDOMIZER

AFTER EFFECTS

Chaos, on tap. A dockable panel that randomizes position, rotation, scale & opacity on selected layers — ranges, add/set modes, and a tidy single-step undo.

Check it out →
🔎

MISSING FILES FINDER

AFTER EFFECTS

Color bars again? Scans your project for missing footage, finds the files anywhere on your machine in milliseconds (Everything search) and relinks them — even PSDs with layers.

Check it out →
