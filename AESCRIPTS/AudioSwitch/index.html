Up and running in 4 steps

No installer, no admin rights, no AutoHotkey needed — it's one self-contained file.

1

Download AudioSwitch.exe

Hit the download button. One file, about 1 MB — drop it anywhere you like.

2

Double-click it

A headphone icon appears in your system tray. That's it — it's now listening for your shortcut.

3

Press F12

The picker pops up at your cursor. Don't like F12? Set your own combo in Settings.

4

Click a device

Your default output switches instantly. Right-click the tray icon for Settings, About, or Exit.

🚀

Want it always ready? Open Settings from the tray icon and tick “Start automatically with Windows.” Now your shortcut works from the moment you log in.

How it works

⌨️

Hit your hotkey

A global shortcut (default F12) pops the picker over whatever you're doing — game, call, DAW, anything.

→
🎛️

See your outputs

Every active output device, each labeled with its adapter — Bluetooth, Realtek, NVIDIA — so identical names are easy to tell apart. No Control Panel.

→
⚡

Click → done

Windows' default output flips immediately. Sound follows you to the device you picked.
