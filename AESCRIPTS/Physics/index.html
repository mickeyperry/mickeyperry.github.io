Install in 3 steps

No Python, no models, no server — it's all JavaScript inside the panel.

1

Download & extract

Hit the download button and extract the ZIP anywhere you like.

2

Double-click install.bat

Enables unsigned panels for your user and copies Physics into the CEP extensions folder. Takes a second.

3

Restart After Effects

Open it from Window > Extensions > Physics — it docks like any native panel.

💡

Changed your mind? uninstall.bat removes it cleanly. Nothing else on your machine is touched.

How it works

🧱

Pick your bodies

Select layers (or take the whole comp). Each becomes dynamic, static, kinematic or dormant, with a collision shape read from its geometry.

→
🎚️

Slide, watch, repeat

Gravity, bounce, friction, walls — every change re-simulates instantly and the preview plays it back at comp frame rate.

→
🔥

Bake

Position & Rotation keyframes land on the real layers, in one undo group. From there it's just After Effects.

Why it's useful

⚙️

Box2D under the hood

The same rigid-body engine Newton is built on, so stacking, sliding, bouncing and sleeping feel right — not "close enough".

🎚️

Sliders, not spreadsheets

Every parameter is a slider with a number box beside it. Drag for the sensible range, type for the wild stuff, double-click to reset, wheel to nudge.

👁️

Live preview

See the whole take play in the panel before anything touches your timeline. Scrub it, sync the AE playhead, click a body to tune it.

🔷

Real shapes

Shape-layer rectangles, ellipses, stars and paths, masks, text and footage bounds — or force box, circle or convex hull per body.

🕹️

Kinematic layers

Already animated a paddle or a conveyor? Mark it kinematic and it follows your keyframes while shoving everything else around.

↩️

Honest keyframes, one undo

No expressions, no hidden effects. Plain Position & Rotation keys you can edit, ease, or delete — and a single Ctrl+Z if you hate it.
